# Independent testing and material use conditions

Version 1.1 — 2026-10-14 · [Українська](TESTING_POLICY.md)

## The same rules for everyone

These rules cover every provider, model, agent, service, tool and participant
in SUPERBENCH evaluations. They apply to testing, comparisons, trial access,
research, feedback and joint evaluations. No provider receives preferential
treatment.

## Independence and authorship

SUPERBENCH documents and may publish, under its own name, its independently
created test cases, methodology, measurements, observations, conclusions and
reproducible evidence, including positive and negative findings. This covers
reports, articles, benchmarks and presentations with appropriate authorship
credit.

Free access, credits, samples or assistance do not grant editorial control or
a right to suppress findings. Providing access alone also does not transfer
rights to our independently created materials.

## Rights and confidentiality

Third-party code, documents, excerpts and model responses are used under their
applicable licenses, agreements and permissions. These rules do not change
existing licenses for public materials or restrict rights they already grant.

We share non-public materials only for an agreed purpose and within an agreed
scope. Public cases and reports must not contain secrets, credentials,
personal data or confidential information. We check anonymization before
sharing: removing names alone does not establish that a material is safe to
share.

## Data sharing, model training and reuse

Before sharing non-public materials, define the permitted purpose, scope,
recipients, retention, deletion and reuse. Testing access alone does not imply
consent to model training, sale or third-party sharing of non-public materials.
Such permissions require separate, explicit agreement. Rights granted by a
public material's license remain in place.

External services also operate under their own terms. Our document does not
override them or establish a provider's acceptance of our requirements. If
terms are unsuitable for a particular material, we do not share it until the
conflict is resolved.

We retain model responses as evidence to review. Receiving an answer does not
make us the author of its text; reuse or publication of third-party content
requires the relevant rights. Our own analysis and reproduction are labeled
separately. Deletion from our storage is not presented as confirmed deletion
by an external service or its partners.

## Result integrity

We distinguish provider claims, returned responses, locally verified facts
and our own conclusions. Completed without a substantive response is not a
usable review. Partial results, missing responses, errors, retries and unknown
costs are reported explicitly. Model names are labeled as provider-reported
unless their correspondence has been independently verified.

A single experiment is not presented as a general quality ranking. Control
experiments do not automatically enter the main leaderboard. Each run has
defined inputs and a methodology version; original materials and results are
preserved. Corrections and reassessments are added as separate revisions.
Demonstrated factual errors are corrected with history retained. Access or a
budget does not imply product endorsement, partnership or confirmation of
safety.

## Provider participation

Before an organized joint evaluation involving non-public materials or access,
the provider and SUPERBENCH agree in writing on the policy version, evaluation
scope, publication rights and specific confidential exclusions. The provider's
representative confirms authority to enter that agreement.

Visiting the repository, using public fixtures or publication of this document
does not constitute automatic acceptance. Conflicting terms must be resolved
before the affected evaluation proceeds. Exceptions require explicit written
agreement by the SUPERBENCH owner.

Suggested acceptance:

> On behalf of [provider], I confirm authority to agree to SUPERBENCH policy
> version 1.1 for [evaluation scope]. SUPERBENCH may publish its independently
> created evaluation materials and findings under its own name, with
> appropriate authorship credit, subject to [confidential exclusions or "none"].
> Conditions for the supplied non-public data: [purpose, recipients, retention,
> deletion and any other permitted uses].

## Status, notices and legal boundaries

SUPERBENCH is an independent research project, not a separate
legal entity. BenkiNew maintains it; each separate agreement identifies the
legal parties and their authority. This policy does not waive
licenses, contractual obligations or third-party rights.

Rights concerns or factual corrections may be raised through the
[maintainer profile](https://github.com/BenkiNew) or repository Issues.
Do not publish secrets or personal data: arrange a private channel before
sending a confidential notice. Identify the disputed material, grounds,
evidence of authority and requested remedy. Verified infringements are
corrected or disputed content is removed from public access, retaining history
only to the extent permitted by law. A notice alone does not prove an
infringement; mandatory legal requirements apply independently of this process.

For separately agreed contracts, the parties expressly specify governing law,
a competent court or another agreed dispute resolution procedure; they may
first attempt written resolution without restricting access to judicial
remedies. This policy alone does not establish jurisdiction over third parties.
Mandatory provisions of applicable law prevail. Invalidity of one provision
does not invalidate the remainder where applicable law permits severability.

Before an external run, we check permissions for automated access, benchmarking
and the intended use of responses, including publication. Where rights are
unclear, results remain private until clarified. Findings are limited to the
stated methodology and do not guarantee product suitability or safety; this
does not exclude liability imposed by law.

## Versions and languages

The Ukrainian and English versions express the same intended policy. If they
differ, the Ukrainian text prevails for this policy; a separate agreement may
expressly select a different controlling language.
Acceptance identifies a specific version and scope; a new revision does not
automatically change earlier agreements. [Version 1.0](docs/policies/TESTING_POLICY.v1.0.md)
is preserved for history.
